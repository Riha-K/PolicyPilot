Refund FAQ
Q: Can I get a refund before the item is returned?
A: No. Refunds are approved only after warehouse inspection, except for approved lost-package claims.

Order Cancellation FAQ
Q: Can I cancel after shipping?
A: Once shipped, cancel is not available. Use the returns flow after delivery instead.

Password Reset FAQ
Q: I did not receive a reset email.
A: Check spam, confirm the account email, then wait 10 minutes before requesting another reset link.
