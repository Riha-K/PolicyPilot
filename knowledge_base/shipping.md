# Shipping Policy

## Standard Domestic Shipping
- Standard shipping for domestic orders typically arrives in **3–7 business days** after dispatch.
- Orders placed before **2:00 PM local warehouse time** are usually dispatched the same business day.
- Weekend and public-holiday orders are processed on the next business day.

## Express Shipping
- Express shipping arrives in **1–2 business days** for most metro areas.
- Express is available only for deliverable ZIP/PIN codes listed at checkout.
- Express fees are non-refundable once the package is handed to the carrier, unless the shipment is lost.

## International Shipping
- International delivery usually takes **7–21 business days**, depending on destination customs.
- Customers are responsible for destination customs duties and import taxes.
- We cannot guarantee delivery dates when customs inspections delay clearance.

## Tracking
- A tracking link is emailed when the order ships.
- Tracking may take up to **24 hours** to show the first carrier scan after the label is created.
- If tracking shows no movement for **5 business days**, contact support with the order ID.

## Damaged or Lost Packages
- Report damaged packages within **48 hours** of delivery with photos of the box and item.
- Lost packages are investigated after **7 business days** past the estimated delivery date.
- Approved claims receive a replacement or store credit within **5 business days**.
