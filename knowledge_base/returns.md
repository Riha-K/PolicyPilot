# Returns and Exchange Policy

## Return Window
- Most items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
- **Electronics** have a shorter return window of **15 days** and require all accessories and seals intact.
- **Apparel** may be returned within **30 days** with tags attached; swimwear and intimate apparel are final sale once the hygiene seal is broken.
- Final-sale / clearance items are marked non-returnable at checkout and cannot be returned.
- The return window starts on the **delivery date** shown in tracking, not the order date.

## How to Start a Return
1. Open **Account → Orders → Request Return**.
2. Select the item and reason for return.
3. Print or show the prepaid return label (where available).
4. Drop the package at an approved carrier location within **7 days** of creating the return.
5. Keep the **RMA number** and drop-off receipt until the refund posts.

If the prepaid label is unavailable for your region, support will email an alternate carrier slip within **1 business day**.

## Refund Timing After Return
- Creating the return does not refund the order by itself.
- Once the warehouse receives and inspects the item, refund approval usually takes **2–4 business days**.
- Refunds then follow the Billing policy timelines for the payment method.
- Store credit (when chosen) usually appears in the account within **24 hours** of approval.
- Original shipping fees are refunded only when the return is due to our error (wrong item, defective, or warehouse damage).

## Partial Refunds
- Missing accessories, damaged original packaging, or clear signs of use may result in a **partial refund** instead of a full refund.
- The inspection note in the return portal explains any deduction.
- Customers can escalate a partial refund decision to support within **7 days** of the note.

## Exchanges
- Size/color exchanges are allowed when stock is available.
- If the desired variant is unavailable, we issue a refund or store credit instead.
- Customers should not ship exchanges without a return authorization number (RMA).
- Exchange shipments use standard domestic shipping unless the customer upgrades at checkout.

## International Returns
- International customers are responsible for return shipping unless the item is defective or incorrect.
- Duties paid on the original shipment are generally not recoverable through us; contact local customs for guidance.
- International return windows still follow the 30-day / 15-day electronics rules above, measured from delivery.

## Non-Eligible Returns
- Used personal-care items, perishable goods, and opened software downloads are not eligible.
- Items damaged by misuse after delivery are not eligible for free returns.
- Missing parts or missing original packaging may reduce or deny refund eligibility.
- Gift cards and digital subscriptions are non-returnable once delivered or activated.

## Defective or Wrong Item
- Report defective or incorrect items within **14 days** of delivery with photos.
- We arrange a prepaid return or, when stock allows, a replacement ship-ahead.
- Approved replacements do not restart the full return window for unrelated items on the same order.
