# Customer Support Guide

## Contact Channels
- In-app chat: average first response under **5 minutes** during business hours.
- Email: support@example-cx.com — responses within **24 hours**.
- Phone: available Monday–Friday, **9:00 AM – 6:00 PM** local support timezone.

## What to Provide
When contacting support, include:
- Order ID
- Account email
- Short description of the issue
- Photos for damage claims

## Escalations
- Tier-1 agents handle shipping status, password resets, and simple return requests.
- Billing disputes and duplicate charges escalate to Tier-2 payments specialists.
- Legal/privacy requests escalate to the compliance team and may take up to **10 business days**.

## Account Security
- A password-reset email can take up to 10 minutes to arrive.
- Never share one-time passwords (OTP) with anyone claiming to be support.
- Official agents will never ask for full card numbers over chat.
- If account takeover is suspected, reset the password and contact support immediately.

## Service Credits
- Courtesy credits may be issued for delayed shipments beyond the promised window.
- Credits are discretionary and usually capped at the shipping fee or a fixed goodwill amount.
- Credits expire **90 days** after issue unless otherwise stated.
