# Billing and Payments Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
- We accept major credit/debit cards, UPI (where available), and approved digital wallets.
- Gift cards can cover part or all of an order balance.
- Cash on delivery may be available for selected domestic PIN codes only.

## Invoices and Receipts
- An invoice PDF is emailed after successful payment capture.
- Customers can download invoices from **Account → Orders → Invoice**.
- Business/GST invoices require tax details to be saved before checkout.

## Refunds
- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** whenever possible.
- Card refunds usually appear in **5–10 business days** after approval.
- Wallet and UPI refunds usually appear in **1–3 business days**.
- Store credit is available as an alternative and is applied instantly.

## Duplicate Charges
- If a customer is charged twice for one order, support can reverse the duplicate after verifying payment gateway logs.
- Provide the order ID and last 4 digits of the payment instrument when requesting a duplicate-charge refund.
- Approved duplicate-charge refunds are processed within **2 business days**.

## Failed Payments
- An authorization hold may appear temporarily even when checkout fails.
- Failed-payment holds typically release automatically in **1–7 business days**, depending on the bank.
- A failed payment does not create a confirmed order.
